# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** July 23, 2026

Amazon Garden Live Sales ("we", "our", or "us") provides software that assists Amazon Garden Thailand in managing live plant sales across supported streaming platforms.

## Information We Collect

When authorized by a manager, the application may access limited information from connected platform accounts, including:

- Public profile information required for authentication.
- Facebook Pages or YouTube channels selected for live streaming.
- Live stream identifiers.
- Public live chat messages associated with authorized live streams.

The application does not collect personal passwords for Facebook or Google accounts.

## How We Use Information

Collected information is used solely to:

- Authenticate managers.
- Connect authorized Facebook Pages and YouTube channels.
- Retrieve live comments during broadcasts.
- Display comments inside the Live Sales Studio.
- Maintain historical records of live sales.
- Improve system reliability and diagnostics.

## Data Storage

Authentication tokens are stored securely on Amazon Garden infrastructure using encrypted server-side storage.

Only authorized administrators and managers may access connected accounts through the Live Sales Studio.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share collected information with third parties except where required to operate authorized platform integrations or comply with applicable law.

## Data Retention

Live comments, session data, and related operational records may be retained for historical reporting, auditing, replay, analytics, and business operations.

Managers may disconnect platform accounts at any time.

## Security

Reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards are used to protect stored information against unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

## Children's Privacy

This application is intended for business use and is not directed toward children.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
