# Data Deletion Instructions

If you have connected your Facebook or Google account to Amazon Garden Live Sales and would like your associated application data removed, please contact us using the information below.

Include:

- Your Facebook or Google account email (or Page name).
- The Facebook Page or YouTube channel connected to the application.
- Any additional information that helps us identify your connection.

After verifying your request, we will:

- Remove stored OAuth credentials.
- Remove saved platform connection information.
- Remove associated application data where applicable, unless retention is required for legal, accounting, or operational recordkeeping.

You may also revoke this application's access at any time from your Facebook or Google account security settings.
